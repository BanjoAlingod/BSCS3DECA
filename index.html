After completing the activities, students should understand the following concepts.

01

Understand Attacks

Identify how brute-force attacks and SQL injection work conceptually.

02

Identify Weaknesses

Recognize weaknesses in authentication and poorly designed web applications.

03

Apply Defenses

Understand techniques such as rate limiting, account lockout, input handling, and parameterized queries.
