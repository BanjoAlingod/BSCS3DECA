🎓 What Should You Look For?

Phishing messages often attempt to manipulate users into revealing information or performing an action.

👤 Sender Check whether the sender's address actually belongs to the organization.
⚡ Urgency Be suspicious of messages demanding immediate action.
🔗 Links Examine where links actually lead before interacting with them.
📎 Attachments Unexpected attachments can be dangerous.
🔐 Credentials Be cautious when a message requests passwords or sensitive information.
✍️ Language Poor grammar, unusual wording, or inconsistent branding can be warning signs.
